# Proposal

## The problem, in one sentence

Finding complete and organized information about Mario Kart games, characters, vehicles, tracks, and items can be difficult when the information is spread across different sources.

## Who it is for

The **Mario Kart Wiki** is for Mario Kart players, fans, and anyone who wants to quickly find information about the games, characters, tracks, vehicles, items, and other Mario Kart content.

## Core features

- Browse information about Mario Kart games.
- View Mario Kart characters and their information.
- View different vehicles and their details.
- Browse race tracks and their information.
- View items and power-ups.
- Organize information into categories for easier browsing.
- Search for specific Mario Kart content.
- Provide a simple and organized interface for accessing information.
- Include images and descriptions to make the information easier to understand.

## Out of scope, and why

- **Online multiplayer** — The project is a wiki and information platform, not a multiplayer game.
- **Online matchmaking** — Matchmaking is unrelated to the main purpose of the wiki.
- **Player accounts** — User accounts are not required for browsing the information.
- **In-game purchases** — The project does not handle purchases or transactions.
- **Real-time game statistics** — The initial version will focus on static information rather than live player statistics.

These features may be considered for future versions if they become necessary.

## Data the app remembers, and where it is saved

The application stores information about:

- Mario Kart games
- Characters
- Vehicles
- Race tracks
- Items and power-ups
- Descriptions and other related information
- Images associated with the wiki entries

The information will initially be stored using **local data or a local database** during development. A backend database may be added later if the project requires online data management.

## Risks

- **Incorrect information** — Wiki information needs to be reviewed to make sure the content is accurate.
- **Copyright issues** — Images and other assets must be properly sourced, licensed, or used according to their permitted usage.
- **Data organization** — A large amount of Mario Kart information may make the wiki difficult to navigate if it is not properly categorized.
- **Outdated information** — New Mario Kart releases or updates may require the wiki content to be updated.
- **Development limitations** — Some planned features may need to be simplified depending on the available development time.
